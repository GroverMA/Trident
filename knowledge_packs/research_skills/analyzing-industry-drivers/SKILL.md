# Industry Drivers and Constraints

Do not produce generic factor lists. Each driver must explain a causal mechanism and distinguish effects on market size from effects on average industry profitability.

## Workflow

1. Scan six directions: demand/customer, technology/product, economics/capital, regulation/policy, supply/value chain, and competition/ecosystem.
2. Convert observations into causal chains: trigger → mechanism → affected actors → measurable intermediate indicator → market outcome.
3. Classify each item as driver, constraint, uncertainty, or conditional factor.
4. Score net impact separately for market size and industry profitability; state direction, magnitude, time horizon, and confidence.
5. Identify leading indicators, thresholds, dependencies, counterforces, and falsification conditions.
6. Separate present structural drivers from future scenarios; pass scenario-dependent claims to Future Intelligence.
7. Resolve contradictions or preserve them explicitly with evidence-quality notes.

## Evidence rules

- Link every material causal step to accepted evidence or mark it as analyst inference.
- Prefer measurable indicators over adjectives such as strong, rapid, or significant.
- Distinguish observed facts, announced plans, forecasts, and inferred effects.

## Hard failures

- Generic PEST-style lists with no causal chain.
- A single impact score that conflates growth and profitability.
- No timing, confidence, indicator, or falsification condition.
- Forecast claims presented as current facts.

## Output contract

Return structured factors, causal chains, dual scores, time horizons, confidence, leading indicators, falsification conditions, evidence IDs, and open uncertainties.
